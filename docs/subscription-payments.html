2 · Subscription life of one property

Pay early or in grace: new period starts after the old end date (no free days). Pay after grace: starts from the payment date.

3 · What is missing

Record from Payments / Renewals built

The Renewals list on the dashboard shows who is due, but you must open each client to record. No button on the row, none on the Payments page.

Fix a wrong payment built

No void or edit. A wrong amount or wrong property keeps the extended plan and the receipt number.

Receipt the client can open built

Only a receipt number is saved. No printable page, no WhatsApp link to send to the hotel.

GST on our own bill decide

Receipt has one amount. No tax split, no GSTIN of ours, no tax invoice for the subscription.

Price list decide

Each property has a free-typed price. No fixed plans, no discounts, no history of price changes.

Duplicate protection done

Same UPI/bank reference cannot be recorded twice. Receipts are numbered per financial year with no gaps.

4 · Collection paths, one engine

Razorpay later plugs into the same step. Nothing built now has to be redone.

6 · Decisions for you

1 · Do we charge GST? needed for task 5

If our turnover needs GST registration, the hotel must get a tax invoice, not only a receipt. Ask your CA before building.

2 · Fixed prices or custom? task 4

Same price list for all hotels, or negotiated price per client. Fixed is simpler to explain and report.

3 · Void rules task 2

Allow void only on the latest payment of a property (recommended), or any payment with a manual date fix.
